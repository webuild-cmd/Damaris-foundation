OUR STORY

The Damaris Foundation for Premature Infants is a dedicated non-governmental organization based in Ibadan, Nigeria, working tirelessly to reduce infant mortality and long-term disability caused by premature birth.

Our mission is deeply inspired by the truth of Job 33:4: “The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.”

Through rapid emergency intervention, critical resource coordination, and professional advocacy, we bridge the gap to high-quality neonatal care for indigent mothers.
